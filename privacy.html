Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

Summary: Møbee is a casual card game. We don't require accounts, don't collect personal information, and don't sell any data. We only store what's needed to make the game work.

1. Information We Collect

Møbee collects minimal information to provide the game experience:

  • Player ID: A randomly generated 4-character code stored locally on your device to identify you in multiplayer games. This is not linked to your identity.
  • Game Preferences: Your selected avatar and last room code, stored locally on your device.
  • Game Session Data: Temporary multiplayer game state (scores, room membership) stored on our game servers during active play sessions.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Payment or financial information
  • Precise location data
  • Photos, contacts, or other device data
  • Advertising identifiers

3. Error Reporting

We use Sentry, a third-party error tracking service, to identify and fix bugs. When an error occurs, Sentry may collect:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser type and version
  • Error messages and stack traces
  • Your randomly generated Player ID (not your real identity)

This data is used solely for improving app stability and is subject to Sentry's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

Local Storage: Your Player ID, avatar selection, and room preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you join a multiplayer game.

Game Servers: During multiplayer sessions, game state is temporarily stored on PartyKit servers. This data is automatically deleted when all players leave a room or after a period of inactivity.

5. Third-Party Services

Møbee uses the following third-party services:

  • PartyKit: Real-time multiplayer infrastructure (Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry: Error tracking and monitoring (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: Typography (Privacy Policy)

6. Children's Privacy

Møbee is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not require account creation or submission of any personal data.

7. Data Retention

Local data (Player ID, preferences) persists until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app. Server-side game session data is temporary and not retained after gameplay ends.

8. Your Rights

You can delete all locally stored data by:

  • Clearing your browser's local storage for this site
  • Uninstalling the app (if installed as PWA)

Since we don't collect personal information linked to your identity, there is no account data to request or delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
